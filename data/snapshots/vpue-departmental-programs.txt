Undergraduate Research in Stanford Departments, Programs, and Research Centers
Many Stanford departments and programs sponsor an undergraduate research program that provides financial support for students to learn from Stanford professors about faculty-designed research projects over the summer quarter.
The departments and programs listed below are funded by the Vice Provost for Undergraduate Education (VPUE) to support undergraduate summer research. This list is not intended to represent all departments, programs, or research centers that offer undergraduate research opportunities at Stanford. Departments and programs may receive funding from other sources and may offer additional research opportunities that are not listed here.
For the VPUE-funded departmental programs listed below, students apply directly to the department or program rather than through VPUE. These programs take place during the summer, and application deadlines vary by department or program. Program details, eligibility requirements, and deadlines are available through the provided links or by contacting the department or program directly.
Don't see your specific area of interest listed below? We encourage you to reach out to the Student Services Officer in the department or program you're interested in. They can help you learn about undergraduate research opportunities and other ways to get involved in research.
