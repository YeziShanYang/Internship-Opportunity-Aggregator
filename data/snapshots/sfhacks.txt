Need to know
Frequently asked questions
Everything you need to know before the weekend begins — and a few things you didn&#x27;t know you needed.
01 What is a hackathon? + A hackathon is a time-bound build sprint. At SF Hacks, you&#x27;ll have about 36 hours to form a team, dream up a project, and build it from scratch before presenting it to our judges.
02 I have no prior hacking experience — can I still participate? + Absolutely. SF Hacks is beginner-friendly, and many of our hackers are first-timers. Mentors, beginner workshops, and starter resources will help you ship your first project.
03 Who can attend, and do I have to know how to code? + All students are welcome, from high school through university. Designers, writers, product thinkers, and first-time coders all have a place on a team.
04 Are there travel reimbursements? + We are not offering travel reimbursements this year. We know travel can be a barrier, so we recommend carpooling with other attendees or connecting on our Discord to coordinate rides.
05 Do I work by myself or with teams? + Either works. You can go solo or team up with as many as four people. If you arrive without a team, we&#x27;ll help you find one at kickoff.
06 Who will I meet at the event? + You&#x27;ll meet student hackers, industry mentors, sponsor representatives, judges, and the SF Hacks organizing team — plenty of people to learn from and build with.
07 Is there a theme for the project? + There&#x27;s no required theme. Build whatever excites you, or choose one of our optional tracks and sponsor challenges for extra direction and prizes.
08 How do I prepare? + Bring your laptop, charger, student ID, and any hardware you want to use. Get some rest, join the Discord early, and show up curious.
09 Is there a code of conduct? + Yes. SF Hacks follows the MLH Code of Conduct, and every attendee, mentor, judge, and sponsor is expected to help keep the event welcoming.
10 Where can I contact you? + Reach us in our Discord, message @sf.hacks on Instagram, or email [email&#160;protected] .
