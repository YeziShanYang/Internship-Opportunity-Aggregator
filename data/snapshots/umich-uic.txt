About The Event
Michigan Interactive Investments hosts the annual ENGAGE® Undergraduate Investment Conference at the Ross School of Business each year with the support of The David Kudla Foundation, among several corporate sponsors. UIC promotes interaction between students across North America and some of the most experienced professionals in the financial services industry through keynote speeches, insightful panels, networking opportunities, and the renowned stock pitch competition.
With legendary investors and professionals, representatives from top firms, and hundreds of bright students, ENGAGE® UIC offers incredible competition, exclusive connections, and recruitment opportunities.
