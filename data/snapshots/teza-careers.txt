Empowering Our Team: Benefits
We believe that our team&#8217;s well-being is paramount. Our benefits package includes:
Medical Plan
United States: Teza offers two medical plans: a PPO and an HDHP with HSA. Both plans cover a wide range of services including office visits, emergency care, and prescriptions.
United Kingdom: Teza provides a Private Health Plan that covers a wide range of services including Private GP consultations.
Armenia: Teza provides Medical coverage through its local health insurance provider in Armenia which allows comprehensive care services.
Vision Plan
United States: Teza provides a vision plan which helps to cover the cost of annual exams, lenses, and either frames or contact lenses.
United Kingdom: Teza provides a cashback coverage option for Optician services through a Private Health Plan.
Armenia: Teza provides Vision coverage through its local health insurance provider in Armenia which allows comprehensive care services.
Dental Plan
United States: Teza provides a dental plan that covers preventive, basic, and major services. The plan also includes child orthodontia coverage.
United Kingdom: Teza provides a cashback coverage option for Dentist services through a Private Health Plan.
Armenia: Teza provides Dental coverage through its local health insurance provider in Armenia which allows comprehensive care services.
Mental Health
United States: Teza offers Mental Health support services through the company’s medical plan.
United Kingdom: Teza offers Mental Health support services through a Private Health Plan.
Armenia: Teza has partnered with a dedicated external provider to offer confidential mental health support to employees.
Retirement Plan
United States: Teza offers a 401(k) retirement savings plan that you can begin contributing to starting with your first paycheck. A variety of investment options are available so you can tailor your savings strategy to your goals. You manage your contributions and investment selections at any time through the plan’s online portal.
United Kingdom: Teza participates in the National Employment Savings Trust (NEST) pension scheme, a government-backed workplace pension program. You are automatically enrolled once eligible and may begin contributing from your first paycheck, with contributions taken directly from your salary.
Armenia: Teza participates in the state pension system, a government-backed workplace pension program. You are automatically enrolled once eligible with contributions deducted directly from your salary.
Lunch Program
United States: Employees may order lunch each day they are working in the Teza office. A delivery service is provided to pay for your lunch order, with a set daily amount available.
United Kingdom: Employees may order lunch each day they are working in the Teza office. A delivery service is provided to pay for your lunch order, with a set daily amount available.
Armenia: Employees may order lunch each day they are working in the Teza office. A payment card for a local canteen is provided to pay for your lunch order, with a set daily amount available.
* All benefits apply to full-time employees only.
* Enrollment for eligible benefits begins immediately upon hire unless otherwise noted.
* All full-time employees are able to elect coverage for themselves and their dependents.
* Local legal and regulatory standards are observed and incorporated into plan design where applicable.
Candidates FAQ
What is the Interview Processes and Timeline?
Our interviews assess both technical and non-technical skills, as both are essential to thrive at Teza. Non-technical skills are evaluated in live interviews, while technical skills are assessed through coding tests, live problem-solving sessions, and technical discussions. Depending on the role, technical topics may include:
Don’t worry
Our recruiting team will share topics in advance so you can prepare.
Fundamental Math
linear algebra, algorithms, problem-solving, and ML basics
Software Engineering
coding, architecture, software design, and SDLC
Data-Related Knowledge
brain teasers, algorithmic logic, and structured reasoning
The average hiring process takes 25-30 days, depending on the role. If we see potential for a better-suited position, we may suggest additional conversations.
How to Succeed at Teza?
Our hiring decisions are always based on combination of different aspects, and still are aligned on Teza’s person success formula:
Success = step(IQ) × step(IT) × step(HR) × exp(Drive)
IQ
Demonstrated through structured problem-solving in math-focused interviews
HR
Demonstrated through clarity, tone, and responsiveness in all communications
IT
Demonstrated through hands-on coding and engineering in technical sessions
Exp(Drive)
Demonstrated through persistence, adaptability, and ownership of outcomes
GOOD TO KNOW
Our process is designed to evaluate your thinking directly. Please refrain from using AI or external tools
What Is the Work Culture
We support scientific, open-door culture with a flatter structure. You’re encouraged to ask questions, share ideas, and collaborate.
While everyone focuses on their tasks, and that is very important, being cross-available to teammates and colleagues helps you understand the company’s business and the inner workings of algorithmic trading better.
What Is My Career Growth
Career paths at Teza offer both horizontal and vertical growth, depending on the role, the team, and impact areas.
We challenge you to grow professionally while providing the freedom to create the best algorithms in finance. Collaboration is fundamental to our success, even as we rely on individual autonomy. At Teza, you’ll be part of a team that values mastery of your craft and having fun while doing it.
Horizontal
Transition to different teams and roles over time if another area also inspires you
Vertical
Deepen your expertise, take on responsibilities, lead teams or projects
What Can I Learn from Recruiters?
You will meet our recruiters before technical interviews to gain more information about the role, the company, and its requirements. We encourage you to take this opportunity seriously by asking as many questions as possible. This is your chance to clarify expectations, learn about our culture, and ensure the role aligns with your career goals.
Remember, we’re flexible and open to accommodating your requests along the way – whether its timelines, interview steps or else. Welcome to Teza!
