LA Hacks
LA Hacks is a 501(c)(3) educational nonprofit that organizes a free, student-focused technology hackathon at UCLA. Our mission is to make hands-on technology education accessible—so students can learn, build, collaborate, and connect with industry professionals.
Our flagship program is the annual 36-hour hackathon at Pauley Pavilion (next: Mid-April 2027), where 1,000+ students build projects with workshops, mentorship, and industry support. We are funded by sponsorships—not donations—and run by student volunteers.
What we do
Our hackathon
Students spend 36 hours developing technology projects while learning new skills, collaborating with teammates, and receiving guidance from mentors and industry professionals—at every experience level.
Workshops
Technical and educational sessions during the hackathon cover software, AI, design, hardware, and more. We also occasionally host workshops and educational events outside the annual weekend.
Mentorship &amp; industry
Mentors help with ideas, debugging, and learning. Companies and professionals join through mentorship, workshops, talks, and judging—connecting classroom skills to real-world practice.

Tracks
Sustain the Spark
Sustain the Spark
Our planet&#x27;s future hangs in the balance, and one bold idea can tip the scales. This track welcomes grassroots innovation and is your space to cultivate projects combating climate change, reducing waste, or accelerating the transition to clean energy. Your project can help the world sustain its spark for years to come.
Catalyst for Care
Catalyst for Care
The pulse of progress beats strongest where it matters the most: human health. This track challenges you to develop solutions that revolutionize the patient-provider experience. From optimizing hospital logistics and streamlining diagnostics to building tools that enhance wellness, your creation will be the catalyst for a healthier world.
Light the Way
Light the Way
A single idea can unlock a universe of knowledge. This track invites you to transform the future of education by making learning accessible to all. You can create projects with accessibility features or interactive learning platforms. How will your spark light up the classrooms of tomorrow?
Flicker to Flow
Flicker to Flow
With all the chaos of the world, how can we turn the flickers into a flowstate? Use this track to focus on enhancing how we work, play, and connect. Whether you automate mundane chores, organize a chaotic schedule, or create a platform that promotes better work-life balance, show us how you can transform friction into function.
Agentverse - Search &amp; Discovery of Agents
Agentverse - Search &amp; Discovery of Agents
Build and Register AI Agents on Agentverse, discoverable via ASI:One, that turn user intent into real, executable outcomes.

What is LA Hacks? LA Hacks is UCLA’s premier annual hackathon - uniting talented students from across the nation for an exciting, 36-hour experience of innovation and collaboration. Participants have a unique opportunity to create groundbreaking projects, connect with like-minded peers, and engage with industry professionals in an unparalleled environment. For more details on what the hackathon is like, check out @lahacks on Instagram!
Who can participate in LA Hacks? LA Hacks is open to college students of all skill levels, from first-time hackers to experienced coders. If you are passionate about technology, creativity, and problem-solving, LA Hacks is the perfect place for you! While LA Hacks is primarily designed for college students, motivated high school students are also welcome to apply.
Where and when does LA Hacks take place? LA Hacks will be hosted at UCLA&#x27;s Pauley Pavilion, the university’s premier basketball and volleyball arena, in Mid-April 2027.
