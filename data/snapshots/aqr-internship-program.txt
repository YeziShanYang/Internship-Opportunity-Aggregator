&nbsp;Important Notice:
Fraudulent Schemes Impersonating AQR
More Info &nbsp;
There have been recent incidents of individuals and groups falsely claiming to be associated with AQR on social media platforms. These imposters have engaged in a variety of unauthorized conduct, including soliciting customers to carry out trading activities with them, impersonating AQR using the genuine names of AQR senior executives or employees to offer investment opportunities, claiming to offer investment training courses that are supposedly provided by AQR or claiming that AQR is backing certain initial coin offerings or is otherwise engaged in crypto related trading activity. There have also been fake websites and mobile applications that claim to be AQR (or associated with AQR) when in fact they are not. These are scams and not authorized by AQR in any way.&nbsp;
Please be aware that AQR does not engage in any investment activities, provide training or financial services, or conduct other regulated activities through social media platforms or messaging apps. Similarly, AQR, its executives and employees do&nbsp; not contact prospects, clients or members of the public via social media platforms or messaging apps such as WhatsApp, Discord and Telegram to offer investment opportunities. AQR does not offer direct investments in cryptocurrencies or other digital assets, it does not provide a trading platform to members of the public, it does not have a mobile app, and it does not offer investment training courses to the public.&nbsp;
AQR and its partners, employees, directors, officers, advisors, successors, and agents are not responsible for any conduct by unauthorized parties and channels, nor are they responsible for the services or information provided by such unauthorized parties and channels. If you have any questions or wish to ascertain whether you are communicating with an authorized representative of AQR, please contact us at https://www.aqr.com/Contact-Us . If you believe that you or someone you know has been a victim of fraud, please report the matter to your local law enforcement or other relevant authorities. For India residents, you may lodge a report at the national cybercrime reporting portal ( https://cybercrime.gov.in ).

Explore Our Internship Program
Our 10-week summer program puts real work of the firm in your hands and gives you the opportunity to truly make an impact.&nbsp; Experience what it&rsquo;s like to work at AQR.
Meaningful Work
You will work alongside AQR employees on real projects for the firm, learning firsthand from some of the best and brightest in the industry.&nbsp;
Professional Skills Development
You will take part in an extensive Quanta curriculum of professional skills led by both internal and external experts. Programs have included professional writing seminars, a series on effective communications and sessions on how to successfully transition from campus to a corporate environment.
Engagement
You&rsquo;ll learn directly from AQR&rsquo;s founders and senior leaders about the firm&rsquo;s history, culture and investment philosophy and gain exposure to all aspects of our business.
Feedback and Mentorship
You&rsquo;ll be supported by both a manager and a mentor with day-to-day guidance and performance feedback.
Social Connectivity
You will have fun and connect with your fellow AQR interns and employees through social events, such as trivia nights, cooking classes, ping pong tournaments and baseball games.
The Intern Experience
Hear from our past interns about their summer experience at AQR.
"From day one, I was encouraged to ask questions, share ideas, and think critically about my project. Through Quanta Academy, I learned about the extensive range of research and innovation happening across AQR, which gave me an appreciation for how ideas connect across the firm."
"Throughout the internship, I met regularly with mentors to brainstorm new directions for my project. I always felt that my ideas were valued, but I was also consistently encouraged to further improve my project. My mentors were incredibly engaged and invested in my growth."
"What stood out most to me was how much AQR supports its interns. The projects are important to the teams, so everyone was genuinely invested in our success providing practical insights into financial markets and offering honest, constructive feedback throughout the summer."
Program Details
Internship applications for the coming summer will be live on our careers page beginning June 30 to mid-August, with interviews taking place throughout September. The ten-week internship begins the first week of June and runs through mid-August.
Learn About Our Teams
Summer interns frequently join the following teams:
Research and Portfolio Management
Summer interns gain hands-on experience in quantitative research and have the opportunity to learn about the investment philosophy that drives AQR. Our summer interns work in collaboration with researchers and portfolio managers, performing statistical and economic research and engaging in the development and implementation of investment strategies.
Portfolio Implementation, Trading, and Portfolio Finance
The Portfolio Implementation, Trading, and Financing team is responsible for the construction, optimization, and management of AQR&rsquo;s systematic portfolios as well as for the efficient trade execution and financing of AQR&rsquo;s investment strategies.
Business Development
The Business Development team works with AQR&rsquo;s clients around the globe, providing solutions to achieve their investment objectives. AQR clients include a cross-section of institutional investors, such as public and corporate pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, foundations and endowments, as well as the private-wealth community and Registered Investment Advisors. Summer interns may work alongside professionals within our US Institutional team or the US Wealth team.
Engineering
Summer interns have the opportunity to help implement new ideas through creative use of technology and see the impact of their work in real-life. Among the teams that Engineering interns may join are the Client Facing Tech Team, which designs and implement projects to support business functions; the Cloud Team, which designs and builds cloud ready applications; or the Middle Office Team, which designs and implements AQR&rsquo;s proprietary systems throughout the firm.
Risk
Summer interns have the opportunity to work alongside and learn from members of AQR&rsquo;s Risk Management team, which has the day-to-day responsibility for managing the market, credit, liquidity, model and counterparty risks of the firm&rsquo;s investments.
