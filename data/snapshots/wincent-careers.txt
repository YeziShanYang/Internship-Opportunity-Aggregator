Culture
The Wincent Way
The ownership is real here.
We are going to become the best by doing things the right way. Every day. It shows up in what we believe, how we work, and what we share.
What we believe
Say things as they are
Life is too short for politics or fluff. If something is wrong, call it out.
Trust each other
Compensation, bonuses, PnL, and strategies are visible internally.
Top players only
Every new hire must raise the bar, or we do not hire.
Integrity is not negotiable
A handshake is sacred. Be the good guys in the market.
Ship fast. Ship often.
A strategy can go from paper to production in a day or two. Speed is the system.
How we work
You own your work end to end. No micromanagement. You figure it out. We support you.
You can
✓ Move forward with imperfect information.
✓ Disagree with the CEO. He will think about it.
✓ Take risks. You are paid to.
✓ Work remotely in select roles and seniorities.
✓ Ask any colleague how anything works. Nothing is siloed.
We don’t
✕ Play politics.
✕ Hide behind process.
✕ Wait days for feedback.
✕ Make the same avoidable mistake twice.
✕ Settle for good enough. The bar rises every quarter.
