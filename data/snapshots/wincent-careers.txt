Culture
The Wincent Way. Ownership Is Real Here.
We are going to become the best by doing things the right way. Every day.
Say things as they are
Life is too short for politics or fluff. If something is wrong, call it out.
Trust each other
Compensation, bonuses, PnL, and strategies are visible internally.
Top players only
One King David beats an army of mediocre soldiers. Every new hire must raise the bar, or we do not hire.
Integrity is not negotiable
A handshake is sacred. Money matters, but not at the cost of our souls. Be the good guys in the market.
Ship fast. Ship often.
A strategy can go from paper to production in a day or two. Speed is the system.
Daily life
These values show up in how we work, day to day. You own your work end to end. No micromanagement. No tutorial on how to make a new strategy profitable. You figure it out. We support you.
It is OK to
Move forward with imperfect information.
Disagree with the CEO. He will think about it.
Take risks. You are paid to.
Work remotely.
Ask any colleague how anything works. Nothing is siloed.
It is not OK to
Wait days for feedback.
Hide behind process.
Protect turf or play politics.
Make the same avoidable mistake twice.
Coast. The bar rises every quarter.
Compensation
Is Public
Every salary, every bonus, and every PnL number is visible internally.
Flat PnL share
Quants and traders get a fixed percentage of their PnL. No politics in how it is split.
Monthly PnL summaries
You see how the business is doing, not just your own work. Department updates, performance context, and PnL summaries are shared internally.
Bonuses twice a year
Team leads and management spend a full week on attribution. We try to be fair. We admit when we get it wrong.
