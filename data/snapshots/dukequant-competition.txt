2026 Duke Trading Competition
March 21-22, Wilkinson Building, Durham, NC
Apply Here
Duke Quantitative Finance is proud to present the second annual Duke Trading Competition, where competitors in teams of two will compete in a variety of events, including live trading on an NCAA game. $5000+ will be donated to charities chosen by the top 3 participants. Award winners will also receive exclusive merchandise from sponsors.
​
The competition will be held at the Duke University campus, from March 21-22. Accepted competitors not in the Duke/UNC area will be reimbursed for flights and housing up to an amount. Meals will be provided. There will also be networking opportunities with representatives of top firms.
​
Applications due January 25, 2026.
