Get ahead with early career programs for students
Students can immerse themselves in life at Capital One ahead of an internship or full-time development program through a variety of bite-sized programs with full-sized impact.&nbsp;&nbsp;
These career programs&mdash;called Summits and Early Internships&mdash;help mostly first-year and second-year undergraduate students develop their skills before pursuing our internships and full-time programs. They vary in length to best meet your skill level, interests and availability. Whatever your experience coming into one of the programs, you&rsquo;ll develop the abilities and confidence to build a successful career.
Students &amp; Grads Summits: Discover an exciting career
Capital One&rsquo;s S&amp;G Summits are two- to five-day events for undergraduate students to meet with leaders and associates, learn what it&rsquo;s like to work here and prepare for future internship or full-time opportunities. The summits are:
Capital One's Analyst Summit: Get insight into what it&rsquo;s like to be a business analyst , a job that helps improve processes and products through strategic consulting. During this two-day event, you&rsquo;ll gain analytical skills, network with business analysts and participate in a live case interview prep workshop to help you prepare for the Analyst Early Internship Program , Analyst Internship Program or Analyst Development Program .
When is the program: May, August&nbsp;
Who we&rsquo;re looking for: First-year students or sophomores pursuing a quantitative/STEM major.&nbsp;
For more info and to indicate your interest: Analyst Early Experiences - AnalystEE@capitalone.com &nbsp;
Capital One&rsquo;s Tech Summit: During this five-day event, you&rsquo;ll participate in workshops on topics like web development, machine learning and hardware&mdash;all important skills to have for your application to the Technology Internship Program or Technology Development Program . The summit culminates with a hackathon and project demos to Capital One leaders.
When is the program: May, August, January
Who we&rsquo;re looking for: Sophomores with beginner coding experience pursuing a computer science/STEM major.
For more info and to indicate your interest: Tech Early Experiences - TechEE@capitalone.com &nbsp;
Capital One&rsquo;s Product Summit: This two-day event will give you an inside look of what it means to be a product manager at Capital One . You&rsquo;ll meet with product managers and learn skills to innovate, influence and drive technology solutions&mdash;important factors for our Analyst Early Internship Program , Product Development Internship Program and Product Development Program .
When is the program: May
Who we&rsquo;re looking for: First-year students or sophomores pursuing a quantitative/STEM related major (economics, engineering, computer science, mathematics, statistics, physics, biology, business, organizational studies, general science, etc.)
For more info and to indicate your interest: Product Experiences - ProductEE@capitalone.com &nbsp;
Capital One&rsquo;s Project Management Summit: Meet and learn from project managers who are leading the way in developing innovative processes and solutions that are at the heart of what we do. You&rsquo;ll come away with skills that will help you in your application to the Management Internship Program or Management Development Program .
When is the program: Varies each year between May-August
Who we&rsquo;re looking for: Sophomores pursuing a major in a subject like economics, information systems, human resources, business administration, finance, marketing, supply chain management, etc.
For more info and to indicate your interest: Enterprise Experiences - Enterprisegradsummit@capitalone.com &nbsp;
Capital One&rsquo;s Finance Summit: Spend two days immersing yourself into our financial teams. You&rsquo;ll network with recent hires and senior leaders, engage in roundtable discussions, participate in skills-building workshops and learn about our Finance Internship Program and Finance Rotational Program .&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
When is the program: May
Who we&rsquo;re looking for: Sophomores pursuing a major in a subject like finance, economics, mathematics, accounting, etc.
For more info and to indicate your interest: Enterprise Experiences - Enterprisegradsummit@capitalone.com &nbsp;
Capital One&rsquo;s Commercial Banking Summit: Get a behind-the-scenes look into what a career in commercial banking means. You&rsquo;ll meet with recent grads and seasoned executives, participate in skill-building workshops, join experiential learning sessions and learn about our Commercial Banking Internship and Commercial Banking Rotational Program .
When is the program: August
Who we&rsquo;re looking for: Sophomores pursuing a major in a subject like finance, accounting, economics, statistics, real estate, etc.
For more info and to indicate your interest:&nbsp; Enterprise Experiences - Enterprisegradsummit@capitalone.com &nbsp;
Early Internship Programs: Gain skills and use them to make an impact
Our 10-week Early Internship Programs are paid, in-person experiences at our McLean office , with corporate housing provided. They offer the opportunity to gain valuable skills as a business analyst. You&rsquo;ll spend the start of the Early Internship Program learning from our skilled associates, then be paired with a team to complete a project that drives Capital One forward.
Analyst Early Internship Program: Become an adept business analyst&mdash;someone who uses data and strategic thinking to improve our company. You&rsquo;ll learn SQL and strengthen your problem-solving skills, generate data-based insights and communicate your findings to stakeholders&mdash;all of which will help you prepare for the Analyst Internship Program or Analyst Development Program .
When is the program: June to August
Who we&rsquo;re looking for : First-year students or sophomores pursuing a quantitative/STEM major.
For more info:&nbsp; Analyst Early Experiences - AnalystEE@capitalone.com &nbsp;
Check out the Students &amp; Grads event page to find info sessions on the programs and connect with the recruiting teams.
