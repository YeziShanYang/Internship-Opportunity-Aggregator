Our Programs and Events
We’re always looking for great new people to join our team. Our programs and events are a chance for us to get to know each other. If you’ve never thought about a career in finance, you’re in good company. Many of us had little to no experience in economics or finance before working here. We look for curious people from any background with a passion for technology and creative problem solving. In other words, people like you.

Graduate Research Fellowship
ACCEPTING APPLICATIONS
Our Graduate Research Fellowship is aimed specifically at PhD students only (not faculty). It is a financial award — one year of tuition and fees plus a $50,000 stipend — to recognize excellence in mathematics, computer science, physics, and statistics. This program supports students while they continue their studies at their home institution.

ACCEPTING APPLICATIONS
This program invites individuals who have just completed their PhD and are about to join a university faculty to conduct research at Jane Street. It's geared toward type system researchers who are excited to work on formal aspects of extensions to OCaml that we've been designing and implementing.
