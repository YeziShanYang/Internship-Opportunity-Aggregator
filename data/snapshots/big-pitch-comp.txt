The BIG Pitch Comp
National Stock Pitch Competition
April 10-11, 2026 at SMU Cox in Dallas TX
Boulevard Investment Group • Hosted at SMU Cox
Competition Overview
A national stock pitch competition for collegiate undergraduates. Finalists will compete in Dallas, Texas at SMU Cox.
Submission (high-level)
Teams submit a pitch deck and a 3-minute thesis video on one U.S.-listed public equity (long or short).
Full submission requirements are available on the application page.

FAQ
Common questions
What do teams submit? +
Teams submit required materials for one U.S.-listed public equity (long or short). See the application page for full rules and submission requirements.
Is lodging provided for finalists? +
Yes. BIG will pay for one hotel night for finalist teams on Friday, April 10 at the Beeman Hotel in Dallas.
Do I need to travel to SMU Cox? +
Yes, all finalists will need to travel to Dallas, Texas for the final round competition at SMU Cox. We have drivers that will escort you from the airport to your hotel.
Who is judging the competition? +
Initial submissions will be judged by BIG Investment Committee members. Finalists will be judged by a panel of investment professionals from sponsoring firms.
