Junior Quant Trader
Chicago Office
The Junior Quant Trader position involves an exciting mix of quantitative analysis, alpha research, and hands-on trading responsibilities. During market hours, Junior Quant Traders actively trade on fast-paced markets with our trading team. After market hours, they work with our quant team to build and improve trading strategies.
Requirements
Bachelor's or advanced degree in math, physics, or similar quantitative field.
Proficiency with python and data analysis libraries (NumPy, Pandas, Sklearn, etc.).
US citizenship or at least five years of US employment authorization.
Gumption.
Full-Stack Quant Developer
Chicago Office
Seeking an experienced, self-motivated problem solver with a versatile skill set to shape the future of Eschaton's technology and strategy. Become a leader at a growing company with a collaborative team environment focused on innovation, creativity, and growth. The Full-Stack Quant Developer oversees Eschaton's software and collaborates with quants and traders to build the next generation of Eschaton's systems and strategies.
Requirements
Mastery of Python.
Web development. Flask and Dash experience preferred.
Linux systems, including task automation, remote server management, and bash scripting.
Database management and SQL. Experience with MySQL and SQLAlchemy preferred.
Complex statistical analysis and backtesting on large datasets using Python (NumPy, Pandas, etc.).
US citizenship or at least five years of US employment authorization.
