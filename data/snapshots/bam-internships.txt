Rotational Programs
Maximize your skillset, jumpstart your career
Successful interns returning to full-time positions in Macro, Commodities, and Technology participate in our rotational programs, which integrate them into top-performing teams across our business. During this time, rotational associates gain broad, hands-on experience and find their best fit for a career at Balyasny.
Fixed Income &amp; Macro
Risk takers
12-month program across two top performing, scaling PM teams.
Commodities
Risk takers &amp; researchers
12-month program across two top performing, scaling PM teams. Upon completion, grads pursue either a risk taking or research track.
Technology
Engineers
