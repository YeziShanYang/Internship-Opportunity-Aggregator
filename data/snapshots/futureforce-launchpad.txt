Define Your Future.

This immersive program takes place in the culture-rich city of San Francisco at the Salesforce Tower, with exposure to the world's #1 AI CRM and how they're transforming businesses with Agentforce.

About Salesforce&nbsp;
Founded in 1999, Salesforce pioneered the revolutionary idea of replacing traditional desktop customer relationship management (CRM) with software in the cloud, making it accessible from anywhere. Today, Salesforce is the world’s #1 AI CRM — where innovation is a constant and humans and agents drive customer success together.&nbsp;
The company is guided by core values — Trust, Customer Success, Innovation, Equality, and Sustainability — to bring out the best in one another and inspire the industry.&nbsp;
Salesforce's University Recruiting strategy is dedicated to cultivating the next generation of talent. Futureforce interns and new grads work on real projects, build a skillset and global network, &nbsp;to propel their careers.

Application Process
There are two stages in the application process. All applicants are reviewed and eligibility is determined for the next stage. Those who successfully pass to the second and final stage must complete two tasks.
Stage 1: Application
Fill out a 15-minute form covering your computer science background, interest in tech, and personal information.
Submit a short answer response.
Requires a free GitHub account.
Stage 2: Technical Task + Interview Task
Complete a project using basic HTML and CSS (3-5 hours).
Respond to interview prompts and submit a 10-minute recording of your answers (1-2 hours)

FAQs
What is a pre-internship?
Futureforce Tech Launchpad provides pre-interns with the technical skillset and workplace experience to successfully apply and succeed as senior college interns. Pre-interns are taught a rigorous Web Development curriculum within a 9 to 5 workplace setting, and walk away with a functioning app that applies their knowledge of full stack web tools.&nbsp;
What is the Futureforce Tech Launchpad program?
Futureforce Tech Launchpad is a full-time 10-week paid summer pre-internship launched by Salesforce and CodePath for rising college juniors. This immersive program offers students a deep understanding of web development, relevant technical work experience, and an opportunity to connect with tech leaders.
During the program, participants will be hired by Salesforce and expected to participate onsite from&nbsp; 9:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m. PT, June 1 - August 7, 2026. The day-to-day experience includes team meetings and daily stand-ups, workshops, and mentorship from software engineering professionals at Salesforce.
This program takes place in person at Salesforce's San Francisco office.
How do I apply to Futureforce Tech Launchpad?
Fill out an application sharing your background in computer science, your interest in a career in tech, and character testimonial. Those who successfully pass the application and make it to the final stage must complete two tasks: a project using basic HTML and CSS, along with a 10-minute recording of your responses to a series of questions.&nbsp;
Who can apply for Futureforce Tech Launchpad?
Students who will complete their sophomore year in Spring 2026 and have plans to major in computer science/engineering&nbsp;at a US college or university are encouraged to apply. Students should also have completed an introductory course in computer science and have familiarity with functions, conditionals, loops, variables, primitive data types, and arrays.
Why should I apply to this program?
The collaboration between Salesforce and CodePath brings students high-quality learning, work experience, and mentorship that will help them shape their education and plan for a career in the tech industry. Students who successfully complete this program may qualify for a Salesforce internship the following summer.
CodePath is a non-profit with years of experience training software engineers at some of the top companies in the tech industry, including Airbnb, Meta, Netflix, and others.
What are the pre-internship dates?
The program runs from June 1 - August 7, 2026.&nbsp;
