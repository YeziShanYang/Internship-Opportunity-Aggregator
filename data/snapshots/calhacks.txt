FAQs
General
+ − What is Cal Hacks? Cal Hacks is a 3-day hackathon, where hackers creatively collaborate on innovative technology for a chance to win prizes including cash, and attend workshops and panels to learn more about emerging tech!
+ − Who are your hosts? We’re Hackathons @ Berkeley, the team behind Cal Hacks and the UC Berkeley AI Hackathon. We ourselves are a team of students wanting to cultivate a space where people can build without boundaries!
+ − Who can participate? Cal Hacks is open to undergraduate and graduate students as well as new grads of under 1 year from all colleges and universities. We have had 300+ universities represented at past editions and are partnering with clubs at other colleges throughout the US.
+ − How many people can be on a single team? Up to 4 people can be part of a single team.
+ − What qualifies as a beginner team? Every team member must be a first-time hacker. Note that attending a prior hackathon and not submitting a project or demoing still counts as having prior experience. We will be verifying with outside sources upon choosing winners.
+ − Do I have to have a team to participate? Not at all! You can come with a team or solo. Whatever works for you!
We will be hosting more relaxed socials before the hackathon to meet like-minded builders. As well as a team matching event during the event itself.
+ − What experience level is needed to attend? All experience levels are welcome!
+ − Can I start on my project before the hackathon? You cannot work on a project you started before the hackathon. Ideation before the hackathon is allowed, but all implementation must occur during the hackathon.
+ − I can’t wait until the weekend to get started at Cal Hacks! We will be launching a whole month of tech talks, workshops, and hack nights leading up to Cal Hacks 13.0! Stay tuned for more information!
+ − Still have questions? Reach out to us at team@hackberkeley.org !
Registration
+ − Who can apply? Cal Hacks is open to undergraduate and graduate students as well as new grads of under 1 year from all colleges and universities. Those who graduated prior to Fall 2025 will not qualify.
+ − How do I apply? Apply at hive.hackberkeley.org by September 13 (priority) / September 20 (regular)!
+ − Why are there two timelines for applying? If you’d like to hear a decision earlier (for planning or financial purposes), the priority deadline is there to ensure you hear back by September 17!
+ − Do I get guaranteed admission if I apply early? No, but chances are much higher!
+ − I applied! What’s next? If you applied during the priority application cycle, you will get one of the following decisions a few days after the deadline: accepted, deferred, or rejected. If you were accepted, you will need to confirm your spot before the provided deadline to attend Cal Hacks 13.0!
+ − If I don’t meet the qualifications to participate, can I volunteer to be a judge or mentor? Yes! Applications can be submitted at hive.hackberkeley.org. Note that judges and mentors must have completed their undergraduate studies to be eligible.
+ − Do you have a waitlist or accept walk-ins on the day-of? Unfortunately, only hackers who were accepted and confirmed by the deadlines will be allowed in the venue. We will not make exceptions. However, we hold multiple events a year — be on the lookout for upcoming events in 2027!
In-Person Logistics
+ − Where is Cal Hacks? Palace of Fine Arts! The schedule and more details will be sent closer to the event!
+ − What should I bring? You should bring a school/gov ID, laptop and chargers, headphones, and anything else you need to stay through the weekend.
+ − Do I have to be in-person? Yes, you and your team will all have to be in-person at the Palace of Fine Arts to participate in the hackathon.
+ − Will food be provided? Yes! Meals and snacks will be provided.
+ − Will there be overnight accommodations? Yes! Hacking all night is fun, but you can also get some rest! We will have a sleeping room in the venue, and there are many local hotels within a 10 minute walk should you opt to book one.
+ − Will my travel be reimbursed? This year, we will be offering travel stipends to a limited number of hackers with regional caps based on the region you are coming from. The application for travel stipend is embedded into the hacker application as an optional question. Choosing whether or not to apply for travel stipend will not affect your chances of being admitted as a hacker. Hackers who are admitted and applied for travel stipend will be informed of their travel stipend decision at the same time as their admission decision. Regional cap amounts will be determined at the same time travel stipend decisions are released.
However, for most attendees, hackers will be in charge of getting themselves to the venue. There’s reliable public transportation in the Bay, such as CalTrain, BART, and buses in San Francisco. We will also be offering a free hacker bus from the BART line to the Palace of Fine Arts for accepted and confirmed hackers – more details to come!
+ − Will there be hardware I can use? Yes! We will provide hardware that you can borrow for your projects.
+ − Will the Wi-Fi work this year? We understand the frustration around the Wi-Fi service at Cal Hacks 12.0 — trust us, we were frustrated too. We are doing everything in our power and making heavy investments to ensure that Wi-Fi runs smoothly this year.
