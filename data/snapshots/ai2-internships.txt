Careers - Internships
View our open roles
If you’re looking to jumpstart your career in AI, our internship opportunities offer world-class collaborators, remarkable freedom, and open access to the right resources so you can tackle breakthrough AI.
Research internships
Undergraduate or graduate students in AI-related fields are invited to apply for full-time research internships with us year-round. All interns are paired with a mentor and will participate directly in Ai2&#x27;s groundbreaking work. There are no restrictions on publications based on internships. International candidates are welcome to apply. Pay is competitive, and visa sponsorship is available.
Engineering internships
Ai2 also offers full-time engineering internships, which are available to junior and senior undergraduates as well as graduate students. Engineering internships might include projects like working on our internal experimentation product to add features for optimizing job scheduling or implementing a recently published model into one of our demos.
FAQs
When are internships offered?
While summer is our primary internship season, we also offer positions year-round. We recommend checking the Careers page frequently and signing up for job alerts .
How long is the internship program?
Ai2 internships are typically 12 weeks long and team members are expected to be on-site at our Seattle office.
No internships are listed, can I still apply?
If no internship roles are currently listed, it means we have filled those positions, and we are not accepting additional applications at this time. Please continue to check the Careers page frequently and sign up for job alerts to get notified about future openings.
When can I apply for a summer internship?
Opportunities for upcoming summer internships are posted in the fall of the previous year. Please check our Careers page frequently and sign up for job alerts to get notified about new openings.
