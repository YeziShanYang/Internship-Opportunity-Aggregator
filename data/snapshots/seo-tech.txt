How it Works
Apply : Candidates complete their online application and submit their resume for review
Skills Profile : Qualified candidates are asked to complete a skills profile designed to capture their current programming skills via Codio
Recruiter Conversation : Candidates connect with a recruiter to ask questions and learn about the SEO Tech Developer program
Pre-Residency : Interns kick-off their summer of programming with part-time individual pre-work which, including comprehensive lessons on Data Structures and Algorithms
Residency : Interns immerse in a virtual six-week summer training, including a stipend totaling $6,000
Interview Prep : Interns take advantage of part-time behavioral and technical training resources including a paid LeetCode subscription to secure an internship for the following summer
Looking Forward : Apply for internships that could lead to full time roles at partner firms and other leading tech companies + support from the SEO Tech Developer team
Application Qualifications
All who meet the following criteria are welcome to apply.
Enrolled as a sophomore at an accredited, four-year US college or university OR students who will be transferring to an accredited, four-year university upon completion of their two-year degree.
Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Statistics, Data Science, MIS, and other quantitative concentrations preferred. All majors considered.
Experience with one or more programming languages is required (Python, Java, C++, etc.).
3.0+ GPA preferred

SEO Tech Developer First-Year Academy is a two part virtual training program during the spring (8-weeks) and summer (4-weeks) , designed to propel first-year college students to the next level.
This program is ideal for students already enrolled in tech-related majors or those who are undecided but interested in exploring computer science.
Our introductory curriculum provides an accessible entry point into the world of coding, giving you a sneak peek into computer science fundamentals
