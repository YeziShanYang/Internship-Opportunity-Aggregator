Hacker Cup is Meta's annual open programming competition. Open to participants around the world, we invite you to apply problem-solving and algorithmic coding skills to advance through each year’s online rounds, win prizes, and have a chance to make it to the global finals and win the grand prize.
In the 2025 Hacker Cup season, we'll allow you to compete in the human division as usual, or in the AI division in which all of your solutions must be written by a computer. We'll open registration on October 3rd, and the practice round will start on October 10th. For more information on the AI track, please join our Discord server .
