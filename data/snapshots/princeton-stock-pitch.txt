About
About the competition
The Princeton Stock Pitch is an intercollegiate stock pitch competition open to
student investment clubs nationwide. Teams present a single long or short idea on a
company with a market cap of $2B+, evaluated over a three-to-five-year
holding period. The competition is held virtually and is free to enter.
01
Submit a pitch
Submit a 3–5 page memo, a presentation, and a supporting model on one long or short idea.
02
Reviewed by judges
A panel of investment professionals reviews every submission.
03
Finalists announced
The strongest teams are selected to advance to the finals.
04
Live Zoom finals
Finalists present live over Zoom to the judges, followed by Q&amp;A.

Requirements
Rules &amp; eligibility
Everything you need to know before you submit.
Who can enter
Open to all student investment clubs. Teams of 3–5 members.
What to submit
A complete submission includes three parts: a 3–5 page written memo, a presentation, and a supporting financial model.
Format &amp; scope
A single long or short idea on a publicly traded company with a market cap of $2B+, evaluated over a 3–5 year holding period.
Presentation
Finalists present live over Zoom to the panel of judges, followed by Q&amp;A.
