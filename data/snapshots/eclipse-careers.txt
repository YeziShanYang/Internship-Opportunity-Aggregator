Careers
Whether you are a graduate looking to start your career in the cutting edge of finance, or an experienced professional looking for your next challenge, we’d like to hear from you.
We’re looking for traders and developers who can work together to solve problems and continuously improve our overall performance.
View all jobs
Experienced Professionals
Eclipse Trading is always looking for experienced hires to join and support our growing teams.
We provide a collaborative environment allowing you to contribute immediately without the overheads and procedures of a larger organisation.
Graduates
Eclipse has a strong commitment to training and supporting our graduates. As a graduate at Eclipse, you will benefit from structured hands-on training, regular feedback and the mentorship of experienced staff.
The success of our graduate program has seen many graduates promoted to senior roles over the course of their careers at Eclipse.
Graduate Trainee Trader
Take part in a rigorous three-month training program at our headquarters in Hong Kong, and after passing your regulatory exams, start trading under the guidance of an experienced trader.
Graduate Developer
Kick start your career as a Graduate Developer by taking part in a two-month rotation across our Quant and Tech teams at our Headquarters in Hong Kong. At the end of your rotation, join one of our development teams and help make changes to our trading systems under the guidance of an experienced software developer.
Software Developer Internship
The Summer internship will take place in our Headquarters in Hong Kong where you will receive on-the-job training with an experienced mentor. Upon completion, you may be considered for a Graduate Software Developer position with us at the end of your studies!
Graduates
Our Graduate Program offer hands-on experience, mentorship and professional development opportunities. If you are a recent graduate seeking valuable industry exposure, our program is tailored to provide you with the necessary skills and knowledge to thrive in your chosen field.
